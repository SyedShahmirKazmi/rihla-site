Qatar Tourist Visa: Requirements, Fees & How to Apply

Whether you need a visa at all, what the Hayya e-visa costs, and the one booking step some nationalities can't skip.

Sourced from Qatar's official Hayya and Visit Qatar platforms. Entry rules change; always confirm your own nationality's requirement on hayya.qa before booking. Last checked: September 2026.

Do you need a visa to visit Qatar?

GCC nationals don't need a visa at all. A passport or GCC ID is enough to cross the border. Beyond that, Qatar's Hayya platform states that well over 100 nationalities are eligible for either visa-free entry or an e-visa issued online in advance, which covers most travelers reading this. The fastest way to check your own passport is Hayya's own nationality tool, since eligibility and stay length vary by country and do change.

The Hayya tourist e-visa (A1)

For nationalities that do need one, the standard route is the Hayya A1 tourist e-visa, applied for online through the official Hayya platform before you fly. Visit Qatar's own visa page lists the fee at QAR 100. Applications are answered through a short questionnaire (your nationality and how you're arriving), and Hayya's mobile app lets you track the application and keep your travel details in one place once it's approved.

Visa on arrival, and the Discover Qatar hotel step

Some nationalities can get a visa on arrival instead of applying ahead of time, also for a QAR 100 fee paid at the airport. The detail worth knowing before you book flights: for a number of nationalities, visa on arrival is conditional on pre-booking an eligible hotel stay through Discover Qatar, the official Qatar Airways travel platform, rather than any booking site. If your nationality falls into that group and you book your hotel elsewhere, you can be turned away at the visa-on-arrival counter. This is worth confirming before you lock in accommodation, not after.

Passport and other entry basics

Visit Qatar's own guidance is that your passport should be valid for at least three months from your date of arrival. Beyond the visa itself, Qatar also requires visitors to hold valid health insurance for their stay. That's a big enough topic on its own that we've written it up separately: travel insurance for a Qatar visit visa.
