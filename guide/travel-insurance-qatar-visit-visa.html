Travel Insurance for a Qatar Visit Visa

Health insurance isn't an optional add-on for a Qatar trip. It's tied to the visa itself. Here's what that actually means.

Sourced from Qatar Insurance Company (QIC) and Visit Qatar's official guidance. Insurance rules and pricing can change; confirm current terms with a Ministry of Public Health-registered insurer before you buy. Last checked: September 2026.

Why insurance is part of the visa, not an extra

Qatar Insurance Company (the country's largest national insurer, and one of the providers registered to sell it) is direct about this: visitor health insurance is a Ministry of Public Health requirement, and without a valid policy you won't be allowed to enter. Visit Qatar's own guidance frames it slightly more softly, as strongly advised cover for Qatar's medical facilities for the length of your stay. Either way, the practical answer is the same: arrive with a valid policy in hand rather than finding out which version applies to your nationality at the border.

What a compliant policy actually covers

QIC's own visitor policy (priced at a fixed, government-approved rate of roughly QAR 50 per month) covers emergency medical treatment and accidents up to QAR 150,000. It's built for the thing visit-visa insurance is meant to catch: something goes wrong unexpectedly, and you're covered for the emergency care. It's explicitly not built to cover pre-existing chronic conditions, so if you're managing one, check that detail with the insurer directly before you rely on it.

Who can sell it, and where to buy it

The policy has to come from an insurer registered with Qatar's Ministry of Public Health. QIC is the one named directly in official guidance, and its visitor policy can be bought online (qic.online/en/visitors) before you travel, in a couple of minutes with just your passport and travel dates. Visit Qatar's own page also notes that visitors without cover already arranged can buy a policy on arrival, but buying ahead means one less queue at the airport.

How this fits the rest of your entry paperwork

Insurance sits alongside the visa itself, not instead of it: see our guide to Qatar tourist visa requirements for the Hayya e-visa process, fees, and the Discover Qatar hotel step some nationalities need for visa on arrival.
