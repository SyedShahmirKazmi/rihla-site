Product

Everything a trip needs, in one companion

Most travel apps make you do the work. Rihla's agent does it with you — and every riyal it quotes comes from curated local data, not guesses.

🗺️

Plan

Chat your way to a costed day-by-day itinerary — transport included, always inside your budget.

💰

Budget

A deterministic engine totals every plan and re-fits it to your target with one tap. The AI never does the math.

🚕

Book

One-tap hand-offs to Uber, Careem, Karwa, Hayya and Q-Tickets — the app opens if installed, the store if not.

🧾

Expenses

Snap a receipt; AI reads the merchant, date and total, and logs it against your trip — originals kept encrypted.

🛂

Visa & Safety

Nationality-aware entry guidance with official Hayya/MOI links, emergency contacts, and opt-in trip sharing.

🎙️

Voice & Memory

Speak your request. Rihla remembers your tastes — vegetarian, traveling with kids — and plans accordingly.

Bilingual by design

Switch to العربية and the entire experience flips — layout, navigation, and the agent itself replies in Arabic. Built for Qatar, not translated for it.

  • Full right-to-left interface
  • Agent conversation in your language
  • Halal and family preferences respected in every plan

Made for the trip, not the desk

A mobile-first experience with offline caching for the moments connectivity drops — souq basements included. Installable today as a web app; native iOS & Android wrap on the roadmap.
