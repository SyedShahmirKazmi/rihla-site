About

Rihla means journey

We are building the travel companion we wanted when friends and family came to visit and we ended up planning their week by hand, in a spreadsheet, on WhatsApp.

Why we built it

Visiting Qatar takes ten apps and a leap of faith

A visitor stitches together search results, itinerary blogs, five booking apps, a currency converter and a visa portal, mostly in English. Ask a general AI planner instead and it gets worse: it invents restaurants, guesses at prices, and never adds anything up.

Rihla answers from a curated list of real Doha venues with checked price ranges, and does the arithmetic in code rather than in the model. If it says your weekend costs 1,480 riyals, that number came from adding up real prices.

What we care about

Three commitments

Honest about money

Prices come from a curated database, totals are computed by code, and where a figure is an estimate we say so. Sponsored placements, if we ever run them, will be labelled.

Arabic as a first language

Right-to-left throughout, and an agent that answers in Arabic rather than translating an English answer. Halal and family preferences are part of planning, not a filter bolted on.

Careful with your data

Documents and receipts are encrypted at rest. Analytics are off unless you turn them on. You can export or erase everything from Settings, and guest data is deleted automatically.
