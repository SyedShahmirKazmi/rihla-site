01 · Problem

Visiting Qatar takes ten apps and a leap of faith

A visitor stitches together search results, generic itinerary blogs, five booking apps, a currency converter and a visa portal, mostly in English only. Generic AI planners make it worse: they invent venues and prices. Nothing speaks Qatar, and nothing is accountable to a budget.

02 · Solution

One conversation, one trusted companion

Rihla plans a costed day-by-day itinerary in one chat, grounded in a curated database of real Doha venues and price ranges, so it never invents. Deterministic budget math, one-tap booking hand-offs, receipt-OCR expense tracking, visa guidance and safety tools. Bilingual English/Arabic with full RTL.

Groundedcurated venue data, no hallucinated prices
Accountableevery plan is totalled and fit to budget by code
LocalArabic-first market; halal & family aware

06 · Competition

Planning is solved. The rest of the trip isn't.

Qatar already has a strong AI trip planner: Visit Qatar's GenAI Concierge, built on Azure OpenAI, plans in 50+ languages and has logged roughly a million interactions. We read that as proof the demand is real. It answers “what should I do?”. The question still unanswered is “can I afford it, did I book it, and what have I spent?”
